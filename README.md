# Herald

A clickable mockup of the one-page life assistant laid out as a morning newspaper.
Seven sections (People, Family, Home & Garden, Arts & Leisure, Business, Scripture, Pulpit),
three primitives (Loop, Log, Backlog), one front page whose headlines are generated from
what is due or drifting.

- Live: https://thekidinside.github.io/herald/
- Map and reasoning: `C:\Artificial\plans\personal-assistant-app-map.md`
- Engine it will sit on: `C:\Artificial\plans\personal-ai-assistant.md` (the Loop model)

Single file, no build, sample data only. Nothing is saved. The "Phone preview" button on
desktop frames the same page at iPhone width; on a real phone the layout switches on its own.

Walkthrough: `docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md`.
